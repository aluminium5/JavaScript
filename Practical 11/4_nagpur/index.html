Famous City Landmarks

Explore the iconic spots that define Nagpur's spirit

☸

Deekshabhoomi

A sacred architectural stupa and world-renowned Buddhist pilgrimage where Dr. B. R. Ambedkar and millions embraced Buddhism.

🌏

Zero Mile Stone

Historic stone monument erected during the Great Trigonometrical Survey of India, marking the exact geographical heart of India.

🌊

Futala Lake

A scenic historic lake built by the Bhonsle kings, famous for vibrant evenings, musical dancing fountains, and authentic local street delicacies.

🏯

Sitabuldi Fort

A prominent hilltop bastion standing as a testament to the 1817 Battle of Sitabuldi, offering panoramic vistas of modern Nagpur city.

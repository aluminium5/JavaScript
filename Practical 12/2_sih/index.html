About SIH

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a nationwide initiative that brings students together to solve real problems given by ministries, departments and industries.

It is organised by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell with AICTE.
Teams build either a software solution or a hardware prototype.

Idea → Team → Prototype → Pitch → National Impact

Software Edition

Web apps, mobile solutions, cloud platforms, and modern AI algorithms built within a high-tempo 36-hour sprint.

Hardware Edition

Physical prototypes, IoT nodes, robotics, and smart sensors with extensive expert mentor support.

Junior Edition

Fostering young school innovators and budding engineers with early-stage guidance and exhibition platforms.

Themes & Challenges

Problem statements are released across diverse critical sectors of the nation. Popular focus categories include:

  1. Smart Agriculture and Sustainable Food Technology
  2. Healthcare, MedTech, and Bio-medical Smart Devices
  3. Clean Energy, Renewable Grids, and Carbon Sustainability
  4. Smart Education, EdTech, and Vernacular Skilling Platforms
  5. Disaster Management, Early Warning Systems, and Intelligent Transportation

💡 Pro Tip: Select a challenge statement aligned with your core passion; it shines through during jury evaluation.
